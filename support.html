Common Issues

  • UDP Network Sync: Ensure your iPad and your Mac running MainStage are on the same local Wi-Fi network or connected via a stable ad-hoc/wired connection.
  • Host FX Mode: Ensure the AUv3 plugin is properly loaded on the channel strip you wish to control. Make sure no firewall is blocking local UDP traffic.
  • Bi-Directional Stutter: Avoid creating infinite feedback loops between your hardware faders and the iPad UI.

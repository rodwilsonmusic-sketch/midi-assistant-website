Built for the moment the song changes

Transitions that never jump.

MainStage has no automation. MIDI Assistant gives you the parts that matter live, and makes them safe.

  • Dynamic Ramps — Linear, Exponential, Log, or S-Curve, timed in bars, beats, or seconds, always starting from the parameter's real current value.
  • Legato Engine — change Scenes mid-chord; sustained notes are carried across and released cleanly.
  • Soft Takeover — a non-motorized fader can't cause a value jump after a Scene change.

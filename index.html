A professional-grade, ultra-low latency MIDI routing and processing hub designed specifically for live performance keyboardists.

The App Store Pitch

Built with a strict lock-free C++ DSP audio kernel, MIDI Assistant guarantees zero UDP stuttering and dropouts when you need it most. Whether you are managing massive parameter dumps from MainStage patch changes, visualizing complex multi-keyboard splits, or applying dynamic velocity curves on the fly, MIDI Assistant gives you total visual and technical control over your rig.

Key Features

  • Host FX Mode (AUv3): Seamlessly integrate into MainStage with a lock-free C++ DSP plugin.
  • Bi-Directional Sync: Your iPad and your hardware are always in perfect harmony.
  • Performance Hub: Visually track splits, layers, and routing for multiple physical keyboards simultaneously.
  • Custom Event Ramps: Design non-linear exponential, logarithmic, and S-curves for precise control over your expression and volume pedals.

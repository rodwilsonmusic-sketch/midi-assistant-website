Data collection and use

MIDI Assistant is a professional audio utility designed to route and process local MIDI data. The apps operate entirely locally on your devices and your local network.

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).

All MIDI routing configurations, Concerts, presets, and network settings are stored locally on your device. Communication between the Mac app and the iPad Remote stays on your local network and is never relayed through any server.
